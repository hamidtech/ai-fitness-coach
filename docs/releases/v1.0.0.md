# AI Fitness Coach 1.0.0

The first public installable Android debug build of AI Fitness Coach, an offline-first personal workout tracker and on-device movement coach.

## Download and install

- Download **AI-Fitness-Coach-v1.0.0-debug.apk** from Assets below.
- Android 8.0 (API 26) or later is required.
- Open the APK on your Android device and allow installation from the selected source if prompted.
- This is a **debug-signed APK**, intended for testing and personal use. It is not a Play Store production-signed build.
- If Android rejects an update of a previously installed debug build because the signing certificates differ, export important workout history before uninstalling or replacing the old installation. Uninstalling deletes local app data.
- The accompanying `AI-Fitness-Coach-v1.0.0-debug.apk.sha256` can be used to verify the downloaded file.

## Highlights

### Workout tracking and camera

- On-device CameraX and MediaPipe pose tracking for every catalog exercise.
- Dedicated form and repetition/hold analyzers for bodyweight squat, dumbbell biceps curl, resistance-band biceps curl and forearm plank.
- Other repetition movements offer experimental, calibrated counting: capture a start pose and an end pose, then repeat within the same visible range. Counts are estimates, with manual correction available.
- Timed holds without a dedicated analyzer use manual timing. Any exercise can continue without camera access.
- Camera setup guidance, skeleton overlay, permission and initialization states, retry actions and offline tracking. Camera frames are processed locally and are not saved or uploaded.

### Training and coaching

- Quick Start, multi-exercise session builder, sets/reps/weights, hold and rest timers, weekly routines and rotating plans.
- Adaptive workout suggestions based on your goal, experience, equipment, approximate session duration and logged performance.
- A recent muscle activity summary for the last 48 hours and a repeatable squat/plank fitness check-in.
- Workout history, progress charts, muscle activity and AI Analysis. Use local rule-based analysis, optional compatible on-device models, or configure an external provider with explicit sharing consent.

### Exercise library and media

- 39 built-in exercises and 1,519 external catalog records after refreshing **catalog v3** (1,558 total).
- Search by exercise, equipment and muscle group. Filter for available video/GIF media or one of 48 mapped Zing guide links.
- Eight verified downloadable MP4 clips in the remote catalog, plus existing licensed images and local media import. Downloaded media stays accessible offline.
- Four new bilingual movements with original diagrams: high plank, child's pose, seated forward fold on the floor and wide push-up.
- In **Exercises**, tap the refresh icon and select **Check for catalog updates** to receive new catalog data and media independently of APK updates.
- Licenses and credits are available in Settings. Zing guides open the original public web pages; Zing media and proprietary SDK are not bundled.

### Personalization and Android integration

- Illustrated progressive profile setup, optional body metrics, English and Persian (RTL), system/light/dark appearance.
- Simple and Advanced navigation, configurable feature visibility, reorganized Settings.
- Health Connect support, optional notifications and two home-screen widgets for daily activity, quick workout and motivation.

## Verification and known limitations

The release workflow publishes the APK from a successful main-branch Android build. The preceding feature verification passed 42 unit tests and 8 Android 15 emulator instrumentation tests, Android Lint with zero errors (120 warnings), and the catalog validator. The emulator also verified a downloaded video checksum and advancing video frames with Wi-Fi disabled and airplane mode enabled.

Exercise-specific form validation remains limited to the four dedicated analyzers. The general pose counter requires visible movement, stable camera framing and calibration; physical-device accuracy across all movements has not yet been validated. Body composition and body-fat estimation from camera images, and a proprietary Zing SDK integration, are outside this release. The app is a workout aid and does not diagnose health conditions.
